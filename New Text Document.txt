            <h1>"Without Truth"</h1>

  	<section class="research-section">

            <p>"Without Truth" is a collection of creative work that are inspired by my research work. 
		Here I explore topics including family, diaspora, queerness, memory, and non-linear narrative, through non-academic writing. <br><br>

		In contemporary culture, truth are often being held as the basis of many important things. 
		People say without truth there's no trust, there's no freedom, there's no relationship. <br>
		However, truths does not exist in the vaccum of power. Who gets to define what is truth? Whose truth gets to be told and heard? <br>
		In the collection of "Without truth", I explore what happens if we accept there is not fixed truths. 
		Rather, we allow perspectives, affects, and desires from different actors entangle together. 
		Feelings are messy. Memories are elastic. So how do we built love, care, and relationship from them?
	    </p>
