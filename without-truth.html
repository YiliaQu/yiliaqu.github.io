"Without Truth"

"“Without Truth” is a collection of creative works inspired by my research. Through creative writing and experimental narrative, I explore questions of family, home, diaspora, queerness, memory, and non-linear storytelling.

In contemporary culture, truth is often treated as the foundation of trust, freedom, and relationships. But truths do not exist outside relations of power. Who gets to define what is true? Whose truth gets to be told, and whose gets to be heard?

In Without Truth, I ask what happens when we let go of the idea of a fixed or singular truth. Instead, perspectives, affects, memories, and desires are allowed to entangle. Feelings are messy. Memories are elastic. So how do we build love, care, and relationships from them?

塑料盆栽 Plastic Plants

《塑料盆栽（Plastic Plants）》 is a mandarin short novel I wrote in 2023. It is about a queer women visiting her old college friend while try to understand her inconsistant memory of college.

It was first published digitally on "月亮纵队 Moon Crew" in September 2023, then published in print in 《剑桥文艺》, a literature magazine run by PhD students in University of Cambridge, in March 2025.

English version coming soon.

The Call

The Call is an interactive novel/Twine game I developed and published in the Summer of 2024.

This game is designed to be an experimental alternative to mainstream Parenting Simulation Games. The player play as a mom who tries to understand the life of her daughter through WeChat.

The development process was supported by NYU DH Graduate Fellowship.
