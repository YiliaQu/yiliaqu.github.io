Research

I am a cultural studies scholar and a digital media researcher with a academic background in gender studies. My research examines broadly around video games and digital media technologies around the broad theme of home. Including: the design of home space, the maintainance of order at home, home as a platform, domestic culture, domestic labor, care work, kinship, family, etc.
